!!! example "Hands-on: Analyzing an AI video generation workflow"

    **Goal:** Examine how a text-to-video system translates a written prompt into visual output.

    **Step 1: Open a video generation tool**

    Use Sora if available, or review [official demonstration examples](https://youtu.be/HK6y8DAPN_0?si=HSHJPbhi4JGXnFZL) if you do not have access.

    **Step 2: Start with a short prompt**

    For example:

    - A short welcome video for library customers
    - A promotional video introducing an AI literacy workshop
    - A short explainer video introducing an ICT topic such as digital platforms, cybersecurity, or user experience design

    **Step 3: Revise the prompt**

    Add more detail about camera view, style, or setting.

    For example, specify the audience, tone, visual style, scene sequence, or whether the video should feel formal, friendly, cinematic, animated, or promotional.

    **Step 4: Compare outputs**

    Consider:

    - Did the system follow the prompt closely?
    - Did motion, perspective, or object consistency break down?
    - What kinds of tasks would this output be suitable for?

    **Step 5: Reflect**

    Why might AI-generated video be useful for ideation but less suitable for situations that require precision or factual accuracy?
